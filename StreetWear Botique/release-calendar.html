How raffles work

No bots, no back doors. Just three steps between you and the pair.

01

Enter

Pick your size and enter online or at the flagship. One entry per person — duplicates are auto-removed.

02

Get drawn

Winners are pulled live on our Friday stream and notified by SMS within the hour.

03

Cop

FAQ

Drop questions

Still stuck? Message the crew.

How do I enter a raffle?

Flip any raffle card and tap Remind me, or enter from the product page. Members get two entries per raffle.

What does “First come” mean?

The pair goes live online at the listed time (ET). Checkout is limited to one pair per customer and protected by bot filtering.

Can I reserve an in-store release?

No holds — in-store releases are wristband-only on the morning of the drop. Wristbands are handed out from 8AM.

Do dates ever change?

Brands occasionally shift dates. We update this calendar within minutes and notify anyone who set a reminder.
