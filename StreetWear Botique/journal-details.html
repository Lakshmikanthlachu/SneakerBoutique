Inside the Friday raffle pull: how we keep it fair

A behind-the-scenes look at bot filtering, live draws and why we publish every entry count.

Rina Okafor

Co-founder · Creative · Oct 9, 2026

Every Friday at 7PM, a few thousand people tune in to watch us pull names. It sounds simple. It isn't. Behind every draw is a week of filtering, de-duplicating and double-checking — because the moment a raffle feels rigged, it stops being a raffle.

Step one: remove the bots

Roughly 38% of entries we receive on a hyped release are automated. We flag them with device fingerprinting, payment hashing and a human review of anything that looks off.

Step two: one human, one entry

Duplicates get merged, not deleted — if you entered online and in-store, you keep one fair shot. Vault members receive a second entry, and we say so on every release page.

“If we can't explain the draw on camera, we don't run it.”
  • Total entries received and removed as automated
  • Pairs available per size
  • Winning entry IDs, anonymised

Transparency is the product. Win or lose, you should know exactly how it worked.
