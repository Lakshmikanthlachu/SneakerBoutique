About KAIROS

Ourstory

From a basement consignment wall to New York's most trusted sneaker boutique — built by collectors, for collectors.

Who we are

We don't sell shoes. We guard the drop.

KAIROS exists because the hunt matters. Every pair is verified by hand, every raffle is drawn in public, and every customer gets the same shot.

Mission

Get the right pair to the right person — at retail, fairly, without bots or backroom deals.

Vision

A sneaker store that feels like a crew: where the drop is an event and the staff are fellow collectors.

Values

Authentic or it's free. Transparent raffles. Respect for the culture that built us.

History

12 years
of heat

Milestones from the basement to the Vault.

  1. 2014

    The basement wall

    Two friends, one consignment wall and 40 pairs in a Canal Street basement.

  2. 2017

    First fair raffle

    We built a bot-proof raffle after watching resellers clear a drop in seconds.

  3. 2020

    Online & same-day

    Launched kairos.demo with same-day bike delivery across Manhattan.

  4. 2023

    The flagship

    6,000 sq ft on Canal St. with a try-on bar, lace station and cleaning lab.

  5. 2026

    Vault membership

    Early access, bonus entries and members-only colourways for 92K members.
