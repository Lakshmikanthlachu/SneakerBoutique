FAQ

Before you visit

Do you buy or consign sneakers?

Yes — consignment intake runs Tue–Thu, 12–6PM. Bring pairs in original boxes; payouts within 7 days of sale.

Can you hold a pair for me?

Regular stock can be held for 24 hours. Raffle and in-store releases cannot be held.

Is there parking?

Street parking is tight. Take the A/C/E or 1 to Canal St — we're a 3-minute walk.
