FAQ

Plan questions

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Monthly plans cancel at the end of the billing period; yearly plans are refundable within 14 days.

Do extra raffle entries guarantee a win?

No — they improve your odds. Every entry is still drawn at random, on stream.

Is same-day delivery available outside NYC?

Same-day covers Manhattan and Brooklyn. Everywhere else in the US ships free 2-day on Insider and Vault.

Can I upgrade later?

Upgrade any time and we pro-rate the difference instantly.
